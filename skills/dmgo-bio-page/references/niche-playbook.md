# Niche playbook

Use this to choose the look for a page. Theme ids come from `dmgo_get_design_options`.
Always pick an accent that contrasts with the theme. If the creator already has a
brand colour, use that.

| Niche | Theme | Template | Font | Accent idea | Hero block |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fitness / gym coach | noir or sunset | masthead | display | #ff5a1f orange | program / 1:1 coaching (product_embed) |
| Yoga / wellness | ocean or mint | portrait | humanist | #14b8a6 teal | batch booking (product_embed) |
| Makeup / beauty / fashion | velvet or blossom | portrait | luxe | #e11d74 rose | masterclass or product |
| Tuition / exam prep | daylight or mint | classic | modern | #2563eb blue | course + free notes (lead_magnet) |
| Doctor / therapist / consultant | daylight or midnight | curve | modern | #0f766e deep teal | consultation booking |
| Finance / trading education | midnight | masthead | grotesk | #22c55e green | course, with reviews right below |
| Food / home chef / baking | sand or sunset | banner | rounded | #ea580c | recipe ebook / workshop |
| Travel / lifestyle | ocean or sunset | banner | editorial | #f59e0b | guide / presets |
| Tech / coding / AI | noir or aurora | spotlight | display | #8b5cf6 | course or community (telegram) |
| Music / gaming / entertainment | grape | neon | display | #a855f7 | membership / tip jar |
| Real estate / local business | daylight | curve | modern | #1d4ed8 | WhatsApp + booking |
| Kids / parenting | bloom or blossom | classic | rounded | #f472b6 | workshop / freebie |

## Layout rules

- Use `neon` only with a dark theme.
- Use `banner` / `portrait` only when there is a good cover or avatar photo.
- Use light themes for trust-heavy niches (health, education, finance for beginners).
- Use dark themes for aspirational niches (fitness, tech, creators-as-brand).
- If the creator has no product yet, the hero becomes the funnel: a `keyword`
  card or `lead_magnet`, plus `dm`.
