# Copy rules

## Display name and category
- Display name: how followers know them ("Coach Aarav", "Dr. Meera Iyer"), not the handle.
- Category: 1–3 words ("Fitness coach", "NEET mentor").

## Bio (max 2 short lines)
Formula: **who I help → result → proof**.
- "I help busy professionals get fit in 20 minutes a day. 1,200+ clients coached."
- "NEET biology made simple. 40k students, 3 years of free notes."

Use proof only if the creator has stated it or the tools return it.
No hashtags. Two emoji at most. No ALL CAPS.

## Button text (ctaLabel, max 40 chars)
Start with a verb, then the outcome:
- "Join the 30-day plan", "Book a 1:1 call", "Get the free notes", "Watch the masterclass"
- Avoid "Click here", "Link", "Buy" on its own, and "Check it out".

## Keyword card
Title: the promise. The keyword shows automatically.
- "Comment PLAN on my reel → get the full workout in DM"

## DM / WhatsApp
- dm title: "DM me on Instagram". message: the keyword or a friendly opener.
- whatsapp title: "Chat on WhatsApp". message: "Hi <name>, I saw your page…"

## SEO
- seoTitle: "<Name>: <what they offer>", max 60 chars.
- seoDescription: one sentence, max 155 chars, with the main offer.

## Tone
Match the creator's own captions. Indian creators often write in Hinglish. Keep
the page in clear English unless asked, and you may mirror their warmth.
